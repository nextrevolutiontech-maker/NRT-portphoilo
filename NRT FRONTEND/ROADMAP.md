# NRT Roadmap (Post-v1.0.0)

With the core engineering architecture frozen, NRT's trajectory shifts strictly to business growth, content authority, and intelligent distribution.

## Quarter 1: The Authority Foundation
- **Target:** Establish unquestionable authority in core sectors.
- **Deliverables:**
  - 50 Flagship Articles (High-depth, intent-driven content).
  - 20 Enterprise Case Studies (Using the strict 10-point architecture).
  - 10 NRT Proprietary Frameworks.

## Quarter 2: Scale & Academy
- **Target:** Broaden the Knowledge Graph and begin structured education.
- **Deliverables:**
  - 100 new Knowledge Assets (Articles).
  - 100 Glossary Terms (To strengthen semantic SEO and graph links).
  - Launch of the NRT Academy (Transitioning from reading to structured learning modules).

## Quarter 3: Distribution & Automation
- **Target:** Maximize the reach and conversion of existing assets.
- **Deliverables:**
  - **Distribution Engine:** Automating the repurposing of 1 MDX article into 10 outputs (LinkedIn posts, PDF summaries, 60-second video scripts, Carousels).
  - **Email Automation:** Lifecycle nurturing based on the Conversion Engine.
  - **AI Assistant Layer:** Launching the 5 core AI Agents (Solution Advisor, ROI Consultant, ERP Architect, AI Automation Expert, Proposal Generator) hooked directly into the NRT Knowledge Graph.

## Quarter 4: Industry Dominance
- **Target:** Become the primary data source for enterprise technology in the MENA and global regions.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Original Research initiatives and surveys.
  - Comprehensive Industry Reports.
  - The Annual NRT Benchmark Index (e.g., The state of ERP implementations in 2026).

## 2027 Vision
Transition the platform from a Knowledge Infrastructure into a fully autonomous SaaS consultancy, leveraging our proprietary Trust Engine and ACI metrics to offer AI-driven digital transformation audits at scale.
