# Next.js Fundamentals for Enterprise

Welcome to the Next.js Enterprise track. In this module, we explore why Next.js is the optimal choice for AI-driven platforms and large-scale applications.

## Why Next.js?

- **Server-Side Rendering (SSR)** for instant load times.
- **Static Site Generation (SSG)** for highly scalable knowledge bases.
- **App Router** for intuitive nested layouts and routing.

In the upcoming lessons, we will build a headless CMS directly inside a Next.js application, mirroring the exact architecture we use at NRT.
