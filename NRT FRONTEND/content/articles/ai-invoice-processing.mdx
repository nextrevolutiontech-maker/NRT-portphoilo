# Automating Accounts Payable with AI

For enterprise finance teams, processing hundreds of vendor invoices every week is a nightmare. It involves opening emails, downloading PDFs, reading the line items, and manually typing that data into an ERP or accounting system like SAP or QuickBooks. It is slow, prone to human error, and a massive drain on skilled labor. 

## The Solution: Intelligent Document Processing (IDP)

Modern AI Automation has solved this via Intelligent Document Processing. This is not the rigid, template-based OCR of the 2010s. Modern AI uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to understand the *context* of a document. 
Even if a vendor completely changes the layout of their invoice, the AI knows that "Total Due", "Amount Payable", and "Balance" all mean the same thing.

## How the AI Workflow Operates

1.  **Ingestion:** An AI agent monitors a dedicated inbox (e.g., `invoices@company.com`). When a PDF arrives, the agent intercepts it.
2.  **Extraction:** The AI reads the document, extracting the Vendor Name, Invoice Date, Tax ID, and every single line item (quantity, unit price, total).
3.  **3-Way Matching:** The AI queries the ERP database to ensure the invoice perfectly matches the original Purchase Order and the Warehouse Goods Receipt.
4.  **Integration:** If everything matches, the AI automatically drafts the journal entry in the ERP, leaving it in a "Pending Approval" state for the CFO to review with one click.

## Financial Impact

By deploying AI invoice processing, businesses typically see a 90% reduction in processing time and completely eliminate late-payment penalties caused by buried emails. Your finance team can stop acting as data-entry clerks and start focusing on cash flow strategy.
