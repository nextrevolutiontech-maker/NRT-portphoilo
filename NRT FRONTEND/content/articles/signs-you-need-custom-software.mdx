# Signs You Need Custom Software

Growth is painful, especially for your IT infrastructure. The off-the-shelf tools that helped you get to $1M in revenue are often the exact same tools preventing you from reaching $10M. Here are the clear signs that your business has outgrown its generic software stack.

## 1. The Spreadsheet Web

If your company's most critical data lives in a shared Excel file with 20 tabs, complex macros, and constant "File is locked for editing" errors, you are at extreme risk. Spreadsheets are not databases; they lack audit trails, user permissions, and relational integrity.

## 2. Double (or Triple) Data Entry

Does your sales team enter a closed deal into the CRM, only for the finance team to manually re-type that same data into the accounting software, and the warehouse team to type it again into the shipping portal? This disjointed workflow guarantees human error and drastically slows down operations.

## 3. Changing the Business to Fit the Software

This is the ultimate red flag. If you have a highly efficient, proven way of processing an order, but your SaaS platform forces you to click through 5 unnecessary screens and use a different terminology just to log the sale, the software is dictating your business model. Custom software adapts to you, not the other way around.

## 4. Unjustifiable Licensing Costs

As mentioned in our Build vs. Buy guide, if your monthly software subscription bills are rivaling your payroll costs simply because you added more users (not because you received more value), it's time to build a proprietary system where you control the licenses.
