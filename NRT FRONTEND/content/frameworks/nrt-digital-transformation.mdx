# The NRT Digital Transformation Framework™

Enterprise software projects fail because they are treated as IT projects rather than business transformation projects. At NRT, we have engineered a proprietary 7-stage framework designed specifically to de-risk software implementations for mid-to-large enterprises.

## Stage 1: Discovery & Bottleneck Identification
We do not start by writing code. We start by auditing your business. We identify the single largest operational bottleneck—the point where your company is bleeding the most money or time (e.g., manual data entry in finance).

## Stage 2: Process Mapping (As-Is vs To-Be)
We document the exact steps your employees take today ("As-Is"). We then engineer the optimized, automated workflow ("To-Be"). If a software system forces you into an inefficient "To-Be" state, it is the wrong software.

## Stage 3: Technology Assessment (Build vs Buy)
We analyze the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO). If the process is a commodity (like email), we recommend buying SaaS. If the process is your competitive advantage, we architect a custom solution.

## Stage 4: Minimum Viable Architecture
We design a modular system. We vehemently oppose the "Big Bang" launch where the entire company switches systems overnight. Instead, we architect a system that can be deployed department by department.

## Stage 5: Implementation & Integration
Our engineers build the solution. Crucially, we build API bridges between the new system and your legacy systems, ensuring zero data silos during the transition period.

## Stage 6: Optimization & UAT
User Acceptance Testing is conducted not by IT, but by the actual end-users (e.g., the warehouse staff). We optimize UI/UX based on their feedback to guarantee 100% adoption.

## Stage 7: Continuous AI Improvement
Once the digital foundation is stable and data is standardized, we deploy Agentic AI to automate cognitive tasks, such as invoice processing and customer support routing, achieving exponential efficiency gains.
