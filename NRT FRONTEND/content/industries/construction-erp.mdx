# Construction ERP: Build with Confidence

Construction projects are notoriously complex, involving multiple sites, hundreds of subcontractors, and volatile material costs. When project managers rely on WhatsApp and scattered Excel sheets, budget overruns and delays are inevitable. NRT delivers Construction ERPs that provide absolute financial and operational control over every project.

## Business Overview

A successful construction firm needs to track profitability not just at the company level, but down to the individual project, phase, and task. Our ERP bridges the gap between the corporate office and the construction site, ensuring that procurement, labor tracking, and billing are always perfectly synchronized.

## Common Problems & Challenges

- **Cost Overruns:** Inability to track actual expenses against the initial project estimation in real-time.
- **Site Inventory Leakage:** Poor tracking of materials like cement and steel delivered directly to remote sites, leading to theft and wastage.
- **Subcontractor Chaos:** Difficulty tracking subcontractor progress, labor compliance, and milestone payments.
- **Fragmented Communication:** The head office and site supervisors operating on different sets of data.

## Core ERP Modules

1. **Project Estimation & Budgeting:** Create detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and track actual costs against the baseline budget.
2. **Site Procurement & Inventory:** Centralized purchasing with localized site-level inventory tracking and material requisition approvals.
3. **Contract & Subcontractor Management:** Track agreements, progress bills, and retention money for all third-party vendors.
4. **Equipment & Asset Tracking:** Manage the location, maintenance schedule, and depreciation of heavy machinery.
5. **Financials & Progress Billing:** Generate accurate invoices based on the percentage of completion (RA Bills).

## AI Automation Opportunities

- **AI Cost Estimation:** Machine learning models that analyze historical project data to provide more accurate cost estimates for new bids.
- **Drone Site Monitoring:** Integrating AI image analysis with drone footage to automatically track construction progress against the CAD models.
- **Automated Invoice Matching:** OCR technology to instantly process and verify supplier invoices against site delivery challans.

## Features & Benefits

- **Mobile Site Supervisor App:** Allow site managers to request materials and log daily labor attendance directly from their phones.
- **Real-Time Profitability Dashboards:** Instantly see which projects are making money and which are bleeding cash.
- **Multi-Company / Multi-Site Support:** Manage several distinct legal entities and dozens of projects from a single unified dashboard.

## Implementation Process

1. **Process Discovery:** We analyze how you currently estimate, procure, and execute projects.
2. **System Design:** Customizing the BOQ and accounting structures to match your standard operating procedures.
3. **Core Build & Pilot:** Implementing the system on a single, controlled pilot project first.
4. **Data Integration:** Importing your historical cost data and vendor databases.
5. **Company-Wide Rollout:** Expanding the system across all active sites with comprehensive field training.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React, with offline-capable mobile apps for site usage.
- **Backend:** Node.js / Python for complex financial calculations and reporting.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL for structured data and scalable cloud storage for project documents/blueprints.
- **Infrastructure:** Highly available cloud architecture ensuring uptime across remote sites.
