# Distribution & Trading ERP: Streamline the Supply Chain

In the distribution and wholesale trading sector, margins are tight and volume is high. If your business relies on fragmented inventory systems and manual order processing, you are bleeding profit. NRT builds robust Distribution ERP systems that automate procurement, optimize warehouse layout, and ensure rapid order fulfillment.

## Business Overview

Distributors act as the critical link between manufacturers and retailers. Success in this industry requires perfect visibility into what you have, where it is, and exactly how much it costs. A modern ERP acts as the central nervous system, connecting your sales teams, warehouse staff, and finance department in real-time.

## Common Problems & Challenges

- **Inaccurate Inventory Levels:** Selling stock that isn't actually in the warehouse, leading to backorders and furious customers.
- **Complex Pricing & Discounts:** Managing different pricing tiers for different B2B customers manually using spreadsheets.
- **Slow Order Fulfillment:** Warehouse pickers spending too much time searching for items, slowing down the shipping process.
- **Lack of Vendor Visibility:** Inability to track which suppliers consistently deliver late or provide defective goods.

## Core ERP Modules

1. **Advanced Inventory Management:** Multi-warehouse tracking, bin locations, and automated stock alerts.
2. **Order Management System (OMS):** Centralized processing for quotes, sales orders, and invoices.
3. **Procurement & Vendor Management:** Automated reordering based on minimum stock levels and historical demand.
4. **B2B E-Commerce Portal:** Allow your wholesale customers to log in, view their specific pricing, and place orders directly into the ERP.
5. **Warehouse Management System (WMS):** Barcode scanning and optimized pick/pack/ship routing.

## AI Automation Opportunities

- **Smart Reordering Algorithms:** AI that analyzes seasonality and market trends to suggest optimal reorder quantities, preventing overstocking.
- **Dynamic Pricing Engines:** Automatically adjust product pricing based on supply chain costs and competitor analysis.
- **Automated Customer Service:** AI chatbots integrated into the B2B portal to handle routine order status inquiries.

## Features & Benefits

- **Mobile Barcode Scanning:** Eliminate picking errors with mobile devices linked directly to the ERP.
- **Real-Time Profitability Analysis:** See the true margin on every order, factoring in shipping and landed costs.
- **Automated Drop-shipping:** Seamlessly route specific orders directly to suppliers for fulfillment.

## Implementation Process

1. **Supply Chain Audit:** We analyze your existing procurement and fulfillment workflows.
2. **Custom Architecture:** Designing a scalable database that handles complex SKUs and pricing matrices.
3. **Phased Development:** Building the core inventory engine first, followed by sales and procurement modules.
4. **Hardware Integration:** Connecting barcode scanners and label printers to the software.
5. **Go-Live & Support:** On-site launch assistance to ensure warehouse operations don't miss a beat.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React, Tailwind CSS for ultra-fast interfaces.
- **Backend:** Node.js, GraphQL for flexible data querying.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL for ACID compliance in financial transactions.
- **Infrastructure:** Scalable cloud hosting with automated backups.
