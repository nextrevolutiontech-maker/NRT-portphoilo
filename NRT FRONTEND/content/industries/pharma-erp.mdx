# Pharma ERP Solution

In the highly regulated pharmaceutical industry, disconnected systems and manual processes are not just inefficient—they are compliance risks. 

Our Pharma ERP integrates manufacturing, quality control, inventory, and distribution into a single, secure platform.

## Business Problems & Pain Points
- High risk of regulatory non-compliance (FDA, GMP).
- Lack of real-time traceability for batch and lot numbers.
- Inefficient inventory management leading to expired raw materials.

## The Solution: Automated Workflows

By moving to a unified architecture, you eliminate silos. 

### Key Modules
1. **Quality Management System (QMS)**
2. **Batch & Lot Traceability**
3. **FEFO Inventory (First Expired, First Out)**
4. **Regulatory Compliance Automation**
