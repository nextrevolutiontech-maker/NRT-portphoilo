# Rice Mill ERP: Optimize Procurement and Yield

Managing a modern rice mill involves complex variables: fluctuating paddy prices, moisture content variations, milling yields, and bulk bag inventory. Standard off-the-shelf software fails to capture these nuances. NRT builds specialized Rice Mill ERPs designed specifically for the unique workflows of the agribusiness sector.

## Business Overview

Profitability in rice milling comes down to two things: buying paddy at the right price and maximizing the head rice yield. Our ERP system tracks every grain from the moment the farmer brings it to the weighbridge, through the drying and milling processes, down to the final packaging and dispatch.

## Common Problems & Challenges

- **Procurement Fraud:** Manual weighbridge slips and moisture readings are prone to manipulation.
- **Poor Yield Tracking:** Inability to accurately track how much milled rice is produced from a specific batch of paddy.
- **Inventory Discrepancies:** Losing track of empty gunny bags, packaging materials, and different grades of rice.
- **Complex Broker Commissions:** Difficulty calculating and tracking payments owed to brokers and commission agents.

## Core ERP Modules

1. **Gate Pass & Weighbridge Integration:** Direct hardware integration with weighbridges to automatically capture gross and tare weights securely.
2. **Paddy Procurement System:** Record moisture levels, deduct quality penalties, and automatically generate farmer/broker payment slips.
3. **Milling & Production Tracking:** Track batches of paddy through drying, milling, and sorting to calculate exact yield percentages (head rice, broken, bran).
4. **Inventory & Bag Management:** Real-time tracking of bulk stock, packaged goods, and empty bag inventory.
5. **Financial Accounting:** Integrated ledger for handling farmer advances, broker commissions, and daily expenses.

## AI Automation Opportunities

- **AI Yield Prediction:** Algorithms analyzing paddy quality parameters (moisture, variety) to predict optimal milling yields.
- **Automated Grading:** Computer vision systems on the sorting line integrated with the ERP to automatically classify rice quality.
- **Smart Procurement Pricing:** AI analyzing market trends to suggest optimal buying prices for raw paddy.

## Features & Benefits

- **Weighbridge IoT Integration:** Eliminate manual data entry and prevent weight manipulation.
- **Real-Time Yield Dashboards:** Monitor milling efficiency and machinery performance instantly.
- **Transparent Farmer Payments:** Generate clear, accurate payment statements to build trust with suppliers.

## Implementation Process

1. **Site Assessment:** We analyze your weighbridge setup, milling plant, and current accounting practices.
2. **Hardware Integration Planning:** Setting up IoT connections for weighbridges and moisture meters.
3. **Custom Module Build:** Developing specific workflows for your regional procurement practices.
4. **Data Migration:** Moving your legacy ledgers and farmer data into the secure new system.
5. **Harvest-Ready Go-Live:** Launching the system with full on-site support during the peak procurement season.
