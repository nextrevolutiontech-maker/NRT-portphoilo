# Textile ERP: Weaving Efficiency into Every Process

Textile manufacturing is uniquely complex, involving continuous processes from spinning yarn to weaving, dyeing, and final garment stitching. Generic manufacturing software cannot handle the intricacies of dye recipes, fabric rolls, and shade variations. NRT designs specialized Textile ERP systems that give you microscopic visibility over your entire production line.

## Business Overview

Profit margins in the textile industry depend heavily on minimizing waste and ensuring strict quality control. Our ERP tracks raw cotton bales, monitors machine efficiency, manages complex chemical dyeing formulas, and oversees the final dispatch of fabric rolls or stitched garments, all in real-time.

## Common Problems & Challenges

- **Waste & Yield Tracking:** Difficulty tracking the exact loss percentage at each stage (spinning, weaving, processing).
- **Dye House Chaos:** Inconsistent color matching and poor management of chemical inventory and dye recipes.
- **Roll / Piece Tracking:** Losing track of individual fabric rolls, their specific lengths, defects, and dye lots.
- **Machine Downtime:** Unplanned maintenance stopping the looms and causing production bottlenecks.

## Core ERP Modules

1. **Spinning & Weaving Production:** Track raw material consumption, loom efficiency, and daily output per shift.
2. **Processing & Dye House Management:** Securely store dye recipes, track chemical consumption, and manage shade matching.
3. **Garmenting & Stitching (Cut-Make-Trim):** Manage pattern cutting, stitching lines, and piece-rate labor calculations.
4. **Quality Control (QC):** Point-based defect tracking system for fabric inspection and automated grading.
5. **Inventory & Roll Management:** Barcoded tracking for individual fabric rolls, yarn cones, and finished garments.

## AI Automation Opportunities

- **AI Defect Detection:** Computer vision systems mounted on inspection machines to automatically detect fabric faults (stains, missed weaves).
- **Smart Dye Prediction:** Machine learning algorithms that suggest slight modifications to dye recipes based on the specific lot of raw material to ensure perfect color matching.
- **Predictive Loom Maintenance:** IoT sensors on looms analyzing vibration patterns to predict part failures before they halt production.

## Features & Benefits

- **IoT Machine Integration:** Connect directly to modern spinning and weaving machinery to capture production data without manual entry.
- **Piece-Rate Labor Calculation:** Automatically calculate worker wages based on the exact number of pieces stitched or inspected.
- **Real-Time Costing:** Instantly calculate the true cost of a finished roll of fabric, factoring in raw material, chemicals, power, and labor.

## Implementation Process

1. **Mill Floor Audit:** A deep dive into your specific processes, from the blow room to the packing department.
2. **Custom Workflow Design:** Tailoring the software to handle your specific units of measure (bales, hanks, picks, meters).
3. **Module Development:** Iterative build process, prioritizing the most critical bottlenecks first.
4. **Hardware Setup:** Deploying touch-screen terminals on the factory floor and integrating barcode printers.
5. **Phased Go-Live:** Rolling out the system department by department to ensure smooth adoption by the workforce.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** React with highly optimized, large-button interfaces for factory floor touchscreens.
- **Backend:** Node.js capable of handling continuous streams of IoT data from machinery.
- **Database:** PostgreSQL for robust data integrity across complex bill of materials (BOMs).
- **Infrastructure:** Hybrid architecture to ensure the factory floor remains operational even during internet outages.
