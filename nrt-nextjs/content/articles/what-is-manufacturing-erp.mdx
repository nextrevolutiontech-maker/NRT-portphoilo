# What is Manufacturing ERP?

Manufacturing businesses operate on razor-thin margins and strict timelines. Relying on disconnected software for inventory, payroll, and accounting inevitably leads to data silos. A Manufacturing Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system solves this by acting as the central nervous system for your entire factory.

## The Problem with Generic Software

Many factories attempt to use generic accounting software (like QuickBooks) combined with massive, complex Excel spreadsheets. The issue? Accounting software doesn't understand a Bill of Materials (BOM). It doesn't know that producing 100 chairs requires exactly 400 legs, 100 seats, and 400 screws, and it cannot automatically deduct these raw materials from inventory the moment a production run starts.

## Core Capabilities of Manufacturing ERP

A true Manufacturing ERP handles:

1.  **Material Requirements Planning (MRP):** Automatically calculating how much raw material to order based on current stock, lead times, and upcoming sales orders.
2.  **Shop Floor Control:** Tracking which worker is operating which machine, and capturing exact labor costs per batch.
3.  **Quality Control:** Enforcing mandatory inspection checkpoints before goods can be moved to the finished goods warehouse.
4.  **Traceability:** The ability to trace a defective finished product back to the exact supplier lot of raw materials.

## Why Custom ERP is Gaining Traction

While legacy systems like SAP or Oracle exist, they are often too rigid and expensive for mid-sized manufacturers. A custom ERP, built specifically for your factory's unique workflows, provides higher ROI by eliminating recurring per-user licensing fees and ensuring 100% adoption by shop-floor workers.
