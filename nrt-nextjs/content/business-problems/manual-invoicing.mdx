While it might seem like a small administrative task, manual invoicing scales terribly. A company processing 50 invoices a month can manage with Excel. A company processing 5,000 invoices a month is hemorrhaging money on manual labor.

When highly paid accountants are reduced to copy-pasting data from a PDF into an accounting system, their strategic value to the business drops to zero.

At NRT, we treat manual data entry as a critical business vulnerability. By implementing AI-driven Intelligent Document Processing, we transform your accounts payable department from a cost center into an automated, error-free machine.
